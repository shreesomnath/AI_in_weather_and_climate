# Typography and Formatting Standards

To ensure the textbook meets professional publishing standards (Word/PDF), all content must adhere to these specifications.

## 1. Typography
- **Font Type**: Times New Roman (all sections).
- **Body Text**: Size 12 pt, Justified, 1.15 or 1.5 line spacing.
- **Page Numbers**: Bottom-center or bottom-right, Arabic numerals.

## 2. Headings & Titles
- **Main Book Title**: 24 pt, Bold, Centered.
- **Subtitle**: 18 pt, Italic, Centered.
- **Chapter Title (H1)**: 20 pt, Bold, Left-aligned or Centered.
- **Section Heading (H2)**: 16 pt, Bold.
- **Sub-section Heading (H3)**: 14 pt, Bold-Italic.

## 3. Typographic Emphasis (Emphasis & Style)
- **Bold**: Use for the first mention of key technical terms, fundamental laws, and critical principles.
- **Italics**: Use for variables in text, specific model names (e.g., *GraphCast*), and for subtle emphasis of key arguments.
- **Underline**: Use sparingly for extreme emphasis or within complex tables to denote hierarchy if bold/italic are already in use.
- **Cross-references**: Italicize references to other chapters or figures (e.g., *See Figure 1.1*).

## 3. Equations & Formulas
- Use **LaTeX** for all mathematical expressions.
- Inline equations: `$E=mc^2$`.
- Block equations: `$$ ... $$` (centered).
- Number all block equations consecutively within chapters (e.g., Eq 1.1).

## 4. Figures & Tables
- **Figures**:
    - Caption below the figure.
    - Style: "Figure [Chapter].[Number]: [Descriptive Title]." (e.g., Figure 1.2: Atmospheric Circulation).
    - Font: 10 pt, Italic.
- **Tables**:
    - Caption above the table.
    - Style: "Table [Chapter].[Number]: [Descriptive Title]."
    - Font: 10 pt, Bold headings.

## 5. Citations & Bibliography
- **In-text**: [Author, Year] (e.g., [Smith, 2023]).
- **Bibliography**: 
    - Required at the end of EVERY chapter.
    - Format: APA or standard scientific format.
    - Dynamic: Must be updated as new references are introduced in the text.
