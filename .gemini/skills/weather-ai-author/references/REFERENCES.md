# Scientific SOTA & Data References

## Key Models to Include
- **GraphCast (Google DeepMind)**: Graph Neural Networks for global weather forecasting.
- **FourCastNet (NVIDIA)**: Adaptive Fourier Neural Operators for high-resolution forecasting.
- **Pangu-Weather (Huawei)**: 3D Earth-specific Transformers.
- **ClimaX (Microsoft)**: Foundation models for weather and climate.

## Essential Datasets
- **ERA5**: ECMWF Reanalysis v5.
- **CMIP6**: Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6.
- **GFS**: Global Forecast System.

## Visual Standards
- Plots should be high-contrast and publication-quality.
- Axes must be clearly labeled with units.
- Captions should explain the "So What?" of the visual.
