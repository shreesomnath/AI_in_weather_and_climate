# Pedagogical Guide for Undergraduate & Graduate Levels

## Undergraduate Focus
- Clear conceptual analogies for atmospheric physics.
- Step-by-step introduction to "What is a Neural Network in the context of a grid cell?"
- Visual aids for spatial/temporal correlations.

## Graduate/Research Focus
- Error propagation and uncertainty quantification.
- Discussion of loss functions: MSE vs. CRPS (Continuous Ranked Probability Score).
- Scalability and computational efficiency of SOTA models.
